# Original assets

`dist/assets/logo.svg` is a custom code-native vector logo: a steaming bowl, an italic Shishi wordmark, spaced FOODZ lettering and a spice-star accent. It is editable and resolution independent.

Food photographs were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then copied into this project. No API CLI fallback was used.

## Hero prompt — dist/assets/biryani.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: hero food photograph cutout for Shishi Foodz, a stylish Tanzanian restaurant website. Primary request: overhead professional food photograph of a large round white ceramic platter filled with authentic Zanzibar chicken biryani, distinct fluffy long basmati rice grains in white and saffron orange, richly spiced browned chicken leg and thigh, crispy caramelised onions, scattered coriander leaves, a lime wedge. Small portion of fresh tomato red onion kachumbari on the plate edge. Entire circular plate visible with comfortable margin. Isolated on genuinely transparent background, natural soft shadow beneath plate only. Mouthwatering, generous, carefully styled yet real street food spirit. Bright warm natural side light, detailed appetising texture. Camera directly overhead flat lay. No text, no hands, no utensils, no tabletop, no extra bowls, no props outside plate. Square composition at high resolution.

## Menu prompt — dist/assets/menu-spread.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: 2 by 2 food photography contact sheet for a Tanzanian restaurant website menu. Four perfectly equal square panels in a 2x2 grid, no gutters, no text or borders. Each quadrant photographed directly overhead, one round ceramic plate centered within its own square on the same warm ivory tabletop, matching natural golden side light and delicious real editorial photography. TOP LEFT: Tanzanian beef pilau, warm brown spiced long grain rice with tender beef chunks, crispy onions and kachumbari, no bright yellow rice. TOP RIGHT: mishkaki, four charcoal grilled Tanzanian beef skewers with beautifully charred edges, lime and fresh salad on an off-white plate. BOTTOM LEFT: chipsi mayai, golden Tanzanian French fry omelette on white plate with fresh tomato kachumbari and thin red chilli sauce. BOTTOM RIGHT: ugali with rich beef stew and dark green sukuma wiki, white maize mound, distinct servings on a plate. Every plate fully within its square. All four dishes appealing with visibly different ingredients and colours. Absolutely no text, logos, watermark, hands, or cutlery.
