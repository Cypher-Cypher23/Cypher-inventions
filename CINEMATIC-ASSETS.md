See [VENUE-ASSETS.md](VENUE-ASSETS.md) for their full prompts, supplied references and saved paths. The earlier concept assets below are preserved for history; the menu photographs remain in use.

The entrance is a three-scene photographic camera sequence with animated zooms and matched dissolves. It is not recorded video or a real-time 3D environment.
